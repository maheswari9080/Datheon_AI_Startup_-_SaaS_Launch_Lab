The most expensive word in the B2B agency business is **"etc."**

Buried under Section 4 of our 20-page proposal PDF was a single innocuous bullet point:  
👉 *"Authentication, profile management, third-party notification integrations, etc."*

Two months into the build, the client asked:  
*"When will the multi-region SMS fallback and custom Salesforce routing be ready? It was in the 'etc.'."*

We faced an agonizing choice:
1. Fight the client and jeopardize our final milestone payment.
2. Absorb 95 hours of unbilled developer overtime to protect the relationship.

We chose #2. Our projected 35% net profit margin collapsed to an 8% loss.
