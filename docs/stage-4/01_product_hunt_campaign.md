### Short Description (Max 260 characters)
> Turn rough client notes into interactive, legally defensive proposals with automated scope creep risk detection, milestone escrow billing, and instant Stripe deposit closing. Built for boutique software agencies and dev studios.

### Long Description
Every agency founder knows the agony: you run an energetic discovery call, and then spend 4 days sweating over a 20-page Google Doc proposal while your competitor swoops in and closes the deal. Even worse, 3 months into the project, the client demands custom integrations that were never budgeted, evaporating your project profit.

**DealForge AI changes the rules of agency deal-making:**
1. **AI Proposal Studio:** Paste raw meeting notes or bullet points; get structured deliverables and milestone pricing in 25 minutes.
2. **Predictive Scope Risk Radar:** Scans your scope for dangerous ambiguity triggers (`real-time`, `sync`, `custom`), scores risk 1–100, and auto-injects protective contingency clauses and margin buffers.
3. **Interactive Client Viewer:** Share a modern, glassmorphic web proposal that clients actually enjoy reading on mobile.
4. **Embedded Stripe Deposit Closer:** Collect upfront project deposits ($1,000–$10,000) directly inside the proposal.
